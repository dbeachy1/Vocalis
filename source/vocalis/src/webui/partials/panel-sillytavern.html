Personas

          
          
          
        

        
Your own personas — the name, picture and self-description SillyTavern injects into the prompt as you. In SillyTavern a persona is not a file — it lives in two maps inside its settings.json — so an existing one arrives by ⟳ Import from SillyTavern. Here it is an ordinary document: ⬆ Import file… takes a .json persona or a .md/.txt write-up, and ✚ New Persona starts an empty one. All three arrive without a face, because a persona avatar carries no data in the PNG the way a character card does; ⬆ Upload PNG under the picture gives them one. Only the description and title are offered to a model. Personas live in Vocalis's encrypted store and every edit mints a new revision.

What Vocalis sends with your instruction 

        

        
        
        
        
        
      

      
Every field you run a pass over is sent as: this system prompt, then the few-shot pairs, then your instruction wrapped in the template below. Saved here and used by every pass from then on — it survives a restart and there is no rebuild. The few-shot pairs are not decoration: they are what stop this model writing ~200 tokens of its own reasoning into your text. If a pass starts pasting its thinking into a field, press Reset.


      
System prompt — the standing instructions, sent first, every time.

      

      
Your instruction, wrapped — {instruction} is what you type; {field} is the field's current text; {context} is the metadata block; {nochange} is the no-change sentinel. {instruction} and {field} are required — without them the model is asked to edit text it was never sent, and it will answer anyway.

      

      
Few-shot pairs — one exchange per block, a blank line between the user half and the assistant half, and --- on its own line between pairs.
