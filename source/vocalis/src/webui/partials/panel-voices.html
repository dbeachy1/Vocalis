Create a voice

Describe a voice and give it a sentence to speak. VoxCPM2 designs it from scratch — no recording needed — then it becomes an ordinary voice the rest of Vocalis clones.
VoxCPM2 isn't loaded — Generate will start it for you (about 11s a card). — optional, to get that wait out of the way first. It holds one card per GPU (~7 GB each) and unloads itself once you've been idle for ten minutes.
Length: a longer reference clones better — 10 s is the floor, around 60 s is ideal, and past 60 s is diminishing returns rather than harm. But VoxCPM2 drifts as one take runs long: by about 36 s clips pick up echo, breath pops and artifacts near the end, and Chatterbox will clone those too.
