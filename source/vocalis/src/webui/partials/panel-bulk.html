Bulk

A one-shot batch pass: drop a pile of files, pick a format, get them back as a single ZIP. Nothing is added to your library — the inputs are throwaway copies and the ZIP is the only thing kept.
Files upload as you pick them and stay here until you run or remove them — picking more adds to the list.
All ticked by default. Untick a card to keep it free (to render on it while the batch grinds through the rest) — the batch only shares the box, so a render you start elsewhere always takes priority and the batch waits, then resumes.
Enhanced output is mono — the AI speech model rebuilds a single voice channel, so stereo isn't available.
The batch is kept until you upload a new one or press Clear. Re-download is free.
