Satellites

A satellite is Vocalis running on another machine, lending its GPU to this one. Its cards appear in Devices above and are used like any other. Speech is rendered there and sent back — nothing is written to that machine's disk, and it never talks to your browser.

The name is cosmetic — it's what the logs and the device rows call it. The URL must match a name in that machine's certificate. Adding one generates a shared secret and shows it once.
